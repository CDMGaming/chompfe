  • Press a knob to flip between its two pages (the dots under it show which). Shift + press resets that page.
  • Shift: with the mode switch down, hold the star key, tap it to latch, or hold your computer's Shift key.
  • Shift + white keys recall sounds 1–14; key 15 is the default sound.
  • Shift + black keys: « » octave, gate 10 / 50 / 100%, pitch and filter LFO on/off, and erase / copy / save. For those three: press it, pick a slot, then press the star key to confirm.
  • Loop: tap LOOP to record, play notes (released keys become steps), tap LOOP again, then PLAY. While it plays, recording replaces the step under the playhead. Hold LOOP to delete the last step; hold PLAY + LOOP to clear.
  • Mode switch up: the star key adds a rest while recording, or mutes the loop while held.

About Chompfe

Chompfe runs the DSP engine of the open-source CHOMPI WAVE firmware by CHOMPI Club (now part of Chase Bliss), compiled to WebAssembly. The engine (voices, wavetable oscillator, DJ filter, LFOs, delay, reverb, compressor, loop sequencer) and the seven factory wavetables come from that release. The controls, step grid and controller support are new.

Chompfe is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by CHOMPI Club or Chase Bliss. CHOMPI is a trademark of CHOMPI Club.

Also built on DaisySP and libDaisy (Electrosmith, MIT) and DSP by Émilie Gillet / Mutable Instruments (MIT).
