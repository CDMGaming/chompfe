About Chompfe

Chompfe runs the DSP engine of the open-source CHOMPI WAVE firmware by CHOMPI Club (now part of Chase Bliss), compiled to WebAssembly. The engine (voices, wavetable oscillator, DJ filter, LFOs, delay, reverb, compressor, loop sequencer) and the seven factory wavetables come from that release. The controls, step grid and controller support are new.

Chompfe is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by CHOMPI Club or Chase Bliss. CHOMPI is a trademark of CHOMPI Club.

Also built on DaisySP and libDaisy (Electrosmith, MIT) and DSP by Émilie Gillet / Mutable Instruments (MIT).
